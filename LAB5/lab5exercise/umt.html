About UMT

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a university in Kuala Nerus District, Terengganu, Malaysia. It was officially chartered on 1 February 2007. The university began as the Centre for Fisheries and Marine Science of Universiti Pertanian Malaysia (UPM) in 1979.

UMT is highly recognized for its specialization in oceanography, marine science, and aquatic resources. It has continuously grown in stature, providing high-quality research and education that focuses on the sustainability of marine and coastal environments, alongside other conventional academic programs.
